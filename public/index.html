How it works

  1. Project

    Per-minute rates from the last 7 days, last 15 days and the season, blended 20% / 30% / 50% and multiplied by projected minutes. Small samples are shrunk toward last season (or league average for rookies), so a hot first week doesn't make a 40-point scorer.

  2. Value

    Each category becomes a z-score against the top 156 players (12 teams × 13), re-selected until the pool is stable. Turnovers are flipped. FG% and FT% use (player% − league%) × attempts, so volume counts. Punt mode drops chosen categories.

  3. Decide

    Start/sit multiplies value by games you can start this week. Waivers weight each category by how much your team needs it. Trades compare team category totals before and after.

  4. Simulate

    10,000 Monte Carlo weeks of the head-to-head: a shared "role shock" per player-game times Poisson counting stats, and shots drawn as attempts then makes. Output: win probability per category, expected score and the swing categories to target.
